What is Thai yoga massage?

An ancient healing practice performed fully clothed on a comfortable floor mat. Using assisted stretches and acupressure, it helps release tension, improve mobility and support the body’s natural energy flow.

More than relaxation: a deeply therapeutic treatment for body and mind. Most clients leave feeling lighter, looser and deeply relaxed.

No prior yoga experience or flexibility is needed. Treatments can be adapted for stiffness, joint discomfort or limited mobility.

Meet Dru

Hi, I’m Dru

A holistic therapist and trainee counsellor in Farnham, supporting women to restore balance, ease tension and reconnect with themselves.

I qualified as a Thai yoga massage practitioner and yoga teacher over 10 years ago, following my own journey through health challenges that deepened my understanding of the body’s ability to heal. I have practised yoga since 2002, with a background in Ashtanga, meditation and mindfulness.

My approach is calm, intuitive and tailored to your individual needs. As a trainee person-centred counsellor, I also bring attentive listening and a safe, confidential space to every session.
