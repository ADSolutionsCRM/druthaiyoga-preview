What is Thai yoga massage?

An ancient healing practice performed fully clothed on a comfortable floor mat. Using assisted stretches and acupressure, it helps release tension, improve mobility and support the body’s natural energy flow.

More than relaxation: a deeply therapeutic treatment for body and mind. Most clients leave feeling lighter, looser and deeply relaxed.

No prior yoga experience or flexibility is needed. Treatments can be adapted for stiffness, joint discomfort or limited mobility.

The meaning behind the symbol

The triskelion: three spirals, one whole

Dru’s emblem is the triskelion, from the Greek triskelḗs, meaning “three-legged”.

Spirals like these turn up across the ancient Greek world, on pottery, coins and mosaics, where they stood for motion, cycles of life and energy flowing outward from a still centre. Three arms turn together around one point, none of them complete without the others.

For Dru, the three spirals are mind, body and spirit. When they move together, we feel whole. That balance is what every session works towards.

I

Mind

Calm, clarity and rest. Each session begins by slowing the breath and letting the mind settle.

II

Body

Release, mobility and ease. Assisted stretches and acupressure work through tension, layer by layer.

III

Spirit

Energy, balance and renewal. Working along the body’s energy lines helps restore its natural flow.

Thai yoga massage in Farnham: your questions

What is Thai yoga massage?

A traditional bodywork practice from Thailand that combines rhythmic acupressure along the body's energy lines with gentle, assisted yoga-style stretches. It eases muscle tension, improves flexibility and leaves you deeply relaxed.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing you can stretch in, such as leggings or joggers and a T-shirt. Thai yoga massage is done fully clothed and without oils.

How long is a session and how much does it cost?

Choose a 1 hour session for £65 or 90 minutes for £85. Your first session is £10 off, and you get £15 off your 5th booking.

Where are you based?

Sessions take place in Farnham, Surrey (GU10), easy to reach from Guildford, Aldershot, Fleet, Godalming and Alton.

When are you open?

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, 09:30 to 14:30. Book online or call 07932 757238.

Is Thai yoga massage suitable for me?

It suits most people, whatever their flexibility. Pressure and stretches are adapted to you. If you are pregnant, injured or have a medical condition, please mention it when booking.
